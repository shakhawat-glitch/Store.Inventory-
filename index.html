Full system access

  • Add, edit and delete Stock In / Stock Out
  • Stock adjustment and reports
  • Manage Data Entry access

Operational data-entry access

  • Dashboard and Current Stock

  • Stock In and Stock Out entry
  • Cannot delete/edit transactions
  • Cannot access administration/settings

👤 Viewer / User

Read-only access

  • Dashboard
  • Current Stock
  • Equipment-wise Stock
  • Summary and Reports
  • Cannot add, edit or delete stock transactions
  • Cannot access administration or settings
